# Decision log

Record decisions that affect the blueprint's behavior, compatibility, or
operational risk. Keep entries concise and link to the relevant proposal or
review when one exists.

## Entry format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Short decision title

- **Context:** What prompted the decision?
- **Decision:** What was chosen?
- **Alternatives:** What other options were considered?
- **Impact:** What does this make easier or harder?
- **Owner / follow-up:** Who will revisit it, and when?
